# דוגמה להרכבה
$$
\begin{gather}
f:\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{R} \\
f\left( x \right) =x^{3}+8 \\
g:\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{R} \\
g\left( x \right) =\sin \left( x \right)  \\
\left( f \circ g \right) \left(x  \right) =f\left( g\left( x \right)  \right) =f\left( \sin \left( x \right)  \right) =\sin ^{3}\left( x \right) +8 \\
\left( g \circ f \right) \left( x \right) =g\left( f\left( x \right)  \right) =g\left( x^{3}+8 \right) =\sin \left( x^{3}+8 \right) 
\end{gather}
$$
