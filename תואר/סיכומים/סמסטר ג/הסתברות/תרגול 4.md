# Exercise ${1}$
Let $X,Y \sim unif\left[ 0,1 \right]$ independently.
We accept that this is the same as $\left( X,Y \right)\sim unif[0,1]^{2}$.
Denote 
$$
	U=\min\left\{ X,Y \right\} , V=\left| X-Y \right| 
$$
### Section A
Find for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$, $F_{u}\left( t \right),F_{v}\left( t \right)$
### Section B
Prove that $\mathbb{P}\left( U=V \right)=0$
## Solution ${1}$
### Section A
Given $0\leq t\leq 1$ we have 
$$
	\begin{gather}
	
	\left\{ \left( x,y \right) :\left| x-y \right| \leq t \right\} ^{c}=\left\{ \left( x,y \right) :\left| x-y \right| >t \right\}  \\
	= \left\{ \left( x,y \right) :x>y+t \right\} \cup \left\{ \left( x,y \right) :y>x+t \right\}  \\
	????? \left\{ \left( x,y \right) :\min\left\{ x,y \right\} \leq t \right\} = \left\{ \left( x,y \right) :x>t \land \right\} 
	\end{gather}
$$

???
### Section B
By definition
$$
\begin{gather}
\left\{ \left( x,y \right) :U\left( x,y \right) =V\left( x,y \right)  \right\} =\left\{ \left( x,y \right) :\min\left\{ x,y \right\} =\left| x-y \right|  \right\}  \\
= \left\{ \left( t,2t \right): t\in \left[ 0,1 \right]   \right\}  \cup \left\{ \left( 2t,t \right) : t \in \left[ 0,1 \right]  \right\} 
\end{gather}
$$
And thus by definition (since it's the area of a set of measure zero) $\mathbb{P}\left( U=V \right)=0$
# Exercise ${3}$
Let $X$ be a random variable
### Section A
Prove that $\forall m\in \mathbb{N}$ there are at most $m$ real numbers $a$ satisfying $\mathbb{P}\left( X=a \right)\geq \frac{1}{m}$
### Section B
Prove that $F_{X}$ has at most countably many discontinuities.
### Section C
Give $X$ such that $F_{X}$ is discontinuous at every irrational and continuous at every irrational
## Solution ${3}$
### Section A
Assume in negation that there  $\exists m\in \mathbb{N},\exists k>m,\exists a_{1}\neq\ldots =a_{k}\in \mathbb{R}$ such that $\forall i\in \left[ k \right].\mathbb{P}\left( X=a_{i} \right)\geq \frac{1}{m}$.
For every $j\neq i \in \left[ k \right]$ $\left\{ X=a_{i} \right\}\cap \left\{ X=a_{j} \right\}= \emptyset$. And thus
$$
	\mathbb{P}\left( \bigcup_{i\in \left[ k \right] }X= a_{i} \right)= \sum_{i\in \left[ k \right] } \mathbb{P}\left( X=a_{i} \right)   \geq \frac{k}{m}\geq 1
$$
Contradiction
### Section B
The discontinuities of $F_{X}$ are exactly
$$
\left\{ x \in \mathbb{R}:\mathbb{P}\left( X=x \right)>0 \right\}= \bigcup_{m\in \mathbb{N}} \left\{ x \in \mathbb{R}:\mathbb{P}\left( X=x \right)\geq \frac{1}{m} \right\}
$$
 which is a countable union of finite sets and thus countable.
### Section C
Define $\Omega=\mathbb{N}, p\left( n \right)=2^{-n}$
There exists a a bijection $f:\mathbb{N}\to \mathbb{Q}$.
Define $X:\mathbb{N}\to \mathbb{R}$ by $X\left( n \right)=f\left( n \right)$.
By construction, $\mathbb{P}\left( X=x \right)>0 \iff x \in \mathbb{Q}$, and thus $F_{x}$ is discontinuous exactly at rationals.
