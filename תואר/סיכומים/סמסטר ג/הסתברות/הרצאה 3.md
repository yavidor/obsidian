# Conditional Probability
## Example
We roll two dice, one <span style="color: blue;">blue</span> and one <span style="color: red;">red</scpan>
### Question ${1}$
What is the prob they sum to ${8}$?
$$
	\Omega =\left\{ \left( \textcolor{blue}{i},\textcolor{red}{j}:1\leq \textcolor{blue}{i}\leq\textcolor{red}{j}\leq 6\right)  \right\} 
$$
