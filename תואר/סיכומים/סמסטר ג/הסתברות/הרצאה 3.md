# Conditional Probability
## Example
We roll two dice, one <span style="color: blue;">blue</span> and one <span style="color: red;">red</scpan>
### Question ${1}$
What is the prob they sum to ${8}$?
$$
	\Omega =\left\{ \left( \textcolor{blue}{i},\textcolor{red}{j}:1\leq \textcolor{blue}{i}\leq\textcolor{red}{j}\leq 6\right)  \right\} 
$$
with the uniform prob. We want
$$
	A=\left\{ \left( \textcolor{blue}{2},\textcolor{red}{6} \right),\left( \textcolor{blue}{3},\textcolor{red}{5} \right), \left( \textcolor{blue}{4},\textcolor{red}{4} \right),\left( \textcolor{blue}{5},\textcolor{red}{3} \right), \left( \textcolor{blue}{6},\textcolor{red}{2} \right) \right\} 
$$
So $\mathbb{P}\left( A \right)=\frac{\left| A \right|}{\left| \Omega \right|} = \frac{5}{36}$
### Question ${2}$
What is the prob. they sum to ${8}$ *given* that the red one rolled a ${5}$.
The options are $\left( \textcolor{blue}{1},\textcolor{red}{5} \right),\left( \textcolor{blue}{2},\textcolor{red}{5} \right),\boxed{\left( \textcolor{blue}{3},\textcolor{red}{5} \right)},\left( \textcolor{blue}{4},\textcolor{red}{5} \right),\left( \textcolor{blue}{5},\textcolor{red}{5} \right),\left( \textcolor{blue}{6},\textcolor{red}{5} \right)$
So the prob is $\frac{1}{6}$
