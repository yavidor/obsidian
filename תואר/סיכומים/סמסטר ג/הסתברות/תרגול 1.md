Two fair dice are rolled independently.
Formally, there exists
$$
	\begin{gather}
	p:2^{\left\{ 1,\ldots,6 \right\}^{2} } \to \left[ 0,1 \right]  \\
	p\left( A \right)  = \frac{\left| A \right| }{36}
	\end{gather}
$$
Denote $X$ as the first number, and $Y$ for the second
# Exercise 1
We'll define
$$
	\begin{gather}
	A=\left\{ \text{The sum of two dice is at least 10} ;X+Y\geq 10 \right\}  \\
	B=\left\{ \text{The two dice show the same number};X=Y \right\}  \\
	C = \left\{ \text{At least one die shows a }6;X=6\lor Y=6 \right\} 
	\end{gather}
$$
Find the probabilities of the following
### Section A
$$
p\left( A \right),p\left( B \right),p\left( C \right)
$$
### Section B
$$
p\left( A \cap B  \right),p\left( A \cap C \right) 
$$
### Section C
$$
	p\left( A \cup B \right) ,p\left( B \cup C \right) ,p\left( A \cup B \cup C \right) 
$$
## Solution 1
### Section A
First let's find what $A$ is
$$
	A=\left\{ \left( 5,5 \right) , \left( 5,6 \right) ,\left( 6,5 \right) ,\left( 6,6 \right) ,\left( 4,6 \right) ,\left( 6,4 \right)  \right\}
$$
Therefore
$$
	p\left( A \right) =  \frac{6}{36} = \frac{1}{6}
$$
And for $B,C$
$$
	\begin{gather}
	B=\left\{ \left( 1,1 \right) ,\left( 2,2 \right) ,\left( 3,3 \right) ,\left( 4,4 \right) ,\left( 5,5 \right) ,\left( 6,6 \right)  \right\} \\\implies p\left( B \right) =\frac{6}{36}= \frac{1}{6} \\ \\
	C=\left\{ \left( 1,6 \right) ,\left( 2,6 \right) ,\ldots,\left( 6,1 \right) ,\ldots \right\}  \implies p\left( C \right) = \frac{11}{36}
	\end{gather}
$$
### Section B
$$
	\begin{gather}
	p\left( A\cap B \right) = \left\{ \left( 5,5 \right) ,\left( 6,6 \right)  \right\} = \frac{1}{18} \\
	p\left( A\cap C \right)  = \frac{5}{36} \\
	p\left( A\cup B \right) = \frac{10}{36}=\frac{5}{18}
	\end{gather}
$$

# Ex. 2
A subset of size ${4}$ is chosen uniformly from $\left[ 10 \right]$
For $i=1,2,3$, denote $A_i$ by the event that $i$ belong to the subset.
### Section A
Calculate $p\left(A_1\right), p\left(A_1\cap A_2\right), p\left( A_{1} \cap A_{2}\cap A_{3} \right)$
### Section B
Calculate $p\left( A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup A_{3} \right)$
### Section C
What is the probability that exactly on of $1,2,3$ is in the set
## Solution 2
### Section A
$$
	p\left( A_{1} \right) = \frac{\binom{9}{3}}{\binom{10}{4} }
$$
$$
	p\left( A_{1} \cap A_{2} \right)  = \frac{\binom{8}{2}}{\binom{10}{4}}
$$
$$
	p\left( A_{1} \cap A_{2} \cap A_{3} \right)  = \frac{7}{\binom{10}{4} }
$$
### Section B
$$
	\begin{gather}
	p\left( A_{1}\cup A_{2}\cup A_{3} \right) = 1-p\left( \left( A_{1}\cup A_{2}\cup A_{3} \right)^{c}  \right)   \\
	= 1-p\left( A_{1}^{c} \cap A_{2}^{c}\cap A_{3}^{c} \right)  = 1- \frac{\binom{7}{4}}{\binom{10}{4} }
	\end{gather}
$$

Another approach is
$$
	\begin{gather}
	p\left( A_{1}\cup A_{2}\cup A_{3} \right)= \frac{\left| A_{1}\cup A_{2}\cup A_{3} \right| }{\binom{10}{4} } = \binom{10}{4} ^{-1} \\
	= \left| A_{1} \right| +\left| A_{2} \right| +\left| A_{3} \right| - \left| A_{1}\cap A_{2} \right| -\left| A_{2}\cap A_{3} \right| -\left| A_{2}\cap A_{3} \right| +\left| A_{1}\cup A_{2}\cup A_{3} \right| 
	\end{gather}
