Two fair dice are rolled independently.
Formally, there exists
$$
	\begin{gather}
	p:2^{\left\{ 1,\ldots,6 \right\}^{2} } \to \left[ 0,1 \right]  \\
	p\left( A \right)  = \frac{\left| A \right| }{36}
	\end{gather}
$$
