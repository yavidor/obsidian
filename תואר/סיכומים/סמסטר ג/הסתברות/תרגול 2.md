# The Two Coin Paradox
We flip two coins such that each pair of outcomes is of equal probability.
### Section A
What is the probability that we got heads twice, given that one of the outcomes is heads
### Section B
Now assume we also choose one of the coins randomly such that each option for outcomes and choice is of equal prob. What is the probability that we got heads twice given that the chosen was heads.
# Paradox Solution
### Section A
First let's define the probability space
$$
	\begin{gather}
	\Omega=\left\{ H H,T T, H T, T H \right\}  \\
	\left( \forall\omega \in \Omega \right)\left( p\left( \omega \right)= \frac{1}{4}  \right) 
	\end{gather}
$$
And now we need to calculate
$$
	\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ HH \right\}|\left\{ H H, H T, T H \right\}   \right)  = \frac{\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ H H \right\} \cap \mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ H H, H T, T H \right\}  \right)  \right) }{\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ H H, H T, T H \right\}  \right) }= 
$$
Notice that $\left\{ H H \right\}\cap \left\{ H H,H T, TH \right\}=\left\{ H H \right\}$, therefore the numerator is $\frac{1}{4}$
From additivity we get that $\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ H H, HT,TH \right\} \right)=\frac{1}{4}\cdot 3= \frac{3}{4}$
Therefore
$$
\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ HH \right\}|\left\{ H H, H T, T H \right\}   \right)  = \frac{\frac{1}{4}}{\frac{3}{4}} = \frac{1}{3}
$$
### Section B
We have
$$
\begin{gather}

	\Omega=\left\{ H H, T T, H T, T H \right\} \times \left\{ 1,2 \right\}  \\
	\left( \forall\omega \in\Omega \right) \left(\mathbb{P}\left( \omega \right) = \frac{1}{8}\right)
\end{gather}
$$
And we want to calculate
$$
	\begin{gather}
	\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ \left( H H,1 \right) ,\left( H H,2 \right)  \right\}| \left\{ \left( HT,1 \right) ,\left( H H,1 \right) ,\left( T H,2 \right) ,\left( H H,2 \right)  \right\}   \right)  \\
	= \frac{\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ \left( H H,1 \right) ,\left( H H,2 \right)  \right\}  \right) }{\mathbb{P}\left( \left\{ \left( H T,1 \right) ,\left( H H,1 \right),\left( T H,2 \right) ,\left( H H,2 \right)   \right\}  \right) } = \frac{\frac{1}{4}}{\frac{1}{2}}= \frac{1}{2}
	\end{gather}
$$
